1. About this policy

Santé Initiative Uganda provides a mobile app for Community Health Workers (CHWs/VHTs), partner outlets and VSLA groups to screen vision, sell reading glasses, track payments and stock, and refer clients to health facilities. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, who sees it, and your choices. The app is not a medical diagnosis tool.

2. Data we collect

TypeWhat we collect
Account data (CHWs, outlets, VSLA members)Full name, phone number (used for one-time passcode login), National ID (for CHW verification), district, village, role, language preference, and documents you upload for group registration.
Client (household member) informationFor each person screened: name, household ID, age, sex, phone number, and district and village. CHWs screen household members of all ages, including infants and children.
Health informationAnswers to the key health questions asked before screening (for example sudden loss of vision, severe headache with eye problems, eye discharge, redness or itching, and diabetes or hypertension); results of the simple eye check, distance vision test and near vision test (pass or fail); reading glasses power dispensed; referrals and referral reasons; follow-up visit notes; and the client's consent. This is sensitive information and we treat it as such.
Financial informationSales amounts (UGX), payment status and due dates, Mobile Money number and transaction ID, and commission. We do not collect PINs or card numbers.
PhotosPhotos only when you choose to take or upload them and the person has given explicit consent.
Device and usage informationAndroid version, app version, and offline and sync logs used to keep the app working.

3. How we use data

  • Register and verify CHWs, outlets and VSLA groups and let you log in.
  • Carry out eye screenings, record results, dispense reading glasses to eligible adults, and create referrals and follow-up visits.
  • Track reading glasses stock, sales and payments.
  • Prepare monthly service reports for supervisors, program management and the Ministry of Health.
  • Keep the app secure, fix problems and support offline use.

We do not use data for advertising and we do not sell personal data.

4. Consent and legal basis

We collect and use data with consent and to run the health program, in line with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019. CHWs must obtain a client's consent before screening and before taking any photo. Clients may refuse or withdraw consent at any time.

5. Who we share data with

  • Health facilities: when you create a referral, the referral details are shared with the facility chosen.
  • Hosting provider: our secure backend servers are hosted with Render, which stores data on our behalf.
  • Service providers: providers that send one-time passcodes and process Mobile Money payments, only to the extent needed to deliver those services.
  • Supervisors: CHW supervisors can see service data from the CHWs they supervise so they can give support and compile monthly reports.
  • Ministry of Health and authorised partners: monthly service reports required for public health programs. Where possible these use aggregated counts that do not identify individuals.
  • Legal requirements: when required by Ugandan law.

We do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for marketing.

6. Offline storage and security

To work without internet, the app stores data on your device until it syncs. Data is sent to our servers over HTTPS and is encrypted at rest on the servers. Access is limited to authorised staff and users. Please keep your phone locked and do not share your login. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

7. Data retention

We keep data while your account is active and for up to 3 years afterwards for program records and medical audit purposes, unless the law requires a different period. After that we delete or anonymise it.

8. Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, or to get a copy of a client's screening history, by emailing info@santeinitiativeug.org with your registered phone number. We will respond within 30 days. Deleting your account removes your personal data except records we must keep by law or for audit, which are deleted at the end of the retention period.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Office in Uganda.

9. Children and minors

The app is used by adult health workers, outlets and group members. Children do not use the app themselves.

CHWs may screen household members of any age. Every person gets a simple eye check with a torch. Distance and near vision tests are done only for people aged 6 and above, and reading glasses are dispensed only to people aged 40 and above. For a child, the CHW records the child's details on the child's own line, with the consent of a parent or guardian, and the parent or guardian gives the history. We collect a child's data only for screening, referral and follow-up, and we do not use it for any other purpose.

10. Data transfers

Our servers may be located outside Uganda. We use providers that protect data with appropriate security safeguards.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and notify users in the app or through an app update when changes are significant.
