## Full Description (4000 characters max)

**Santé Initiative Uganda** is a comprehensive mobile health application designed to empower Village Health Teams (VHTs) in rural Uganda to provide accessible vision care services.

### Key Features

🔍 **Vision Screening**
- Comprehensive eye health assessments
- Visual acuity testing
- Symptom tracking and documentation
- Photo documentation of eye conditions
- Offline-capable screening forms

👥 **Client Management**
- Register and track clients
- Maintain detailed health records
- Track screening history
- Monitor treatment progress

👓 **Eyewear Distribution**
- Browse available eyewear products
- Check stock levels in real-time
- Process orders and distributions
- Track inventory

💰 **Flexible Payment Options**
- Mobile Money integration
- Installment payment plans
- Payment tracking and receipts
- Financial reporting

🏥 **Referral System**
- Identify cases requiring specialist care
- Generate referral documentation
- Track referral outcomes
- Connect with healthcare facilities

📊 **Dashboard & Analytics**
- Real-time statistics
- Performance metrics
- Inventory management
- Client insights

🌐 **Multi-Language Support**
- English
- Luganda
- Runyankole
- Ateso
- Luo

📴 **Offline Functionality**
- Work without internet connection
- Automatic data synchronization
- Queue offline actions
- Reliable in rural areas

### Who Is This For?

- Village Health Teams (VHTs)
- Community Health Workers
- Eye Care Professionals
- Health Program Coordinators
- Rural Health Initiatives

### Our Mission

Santé Initiative Uganda aims to bridge the gap in vision care accessibility in rural communities. By equipping VHTs with modern tools, we're making quality eye care available to underserved populations across Uganda.

### Security & Privacy

- Secure authentication with OTP verification
- Encrypted data transmission
- HIPAA-compliant data handling
- Role-based access control
